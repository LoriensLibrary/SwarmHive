**A second surface template.** Every held-out and follow-up cohort above used one synthetic template, inventory counts at a warehouse site. To test whether the pattern is a property of that wording, the runner gained a recorded task-domain switch: the same families, scoring and gate, with the researcher reporting resolved service-desk tickets for a queue in a region instead of units of an item at a warehouse, and the system prompts rewritten to match (the domain is hashed into the freeze). Two post-hoc cohorts, new seeds, 30 instances per family, lookup optional as frozen: gemma-4-31b-it: preloaded lineage 60 of 60, lookup offered 31 of 60 with the lookup taken 2 of 60; unsafe commits 0; deepseek-v4-flash: preloaded lineage 21 of 21, lookup offered 20 of 22 with the lookup taken 20 of 22; unsafe commits 0 (cohort still running at the time of writing). The ordering is the one the inventory template produced: preloaded lineage completes the same-number case, an optional lookup completes it only as often as the model takes it, and the gate commits nothing unsafe.
